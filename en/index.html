Relic Sweeper

Minesweeper without guessing, and a museum to complete.

Dig tiles like in classic minesweeper, but with no guessing: every board can be solved by logic. Recovered fragments become exhibits, and exhibits fill the cabinets of your museum. Expeditions to Egypt and Pompeii, a daily dig and a free mode.

iPhone, iOS 26 or later.
