Relic Sweeper Privacy Policy

Effective: 8 November 2026

Who is responsible

The controller is Arkadiusz Pituła, contact: pebblelogic.games@gmail.com.

Your progress stays on your device

Your museum, tickets, settings and unfinished digs are stored only on your device. There are no user accounts and no server of ours holding your data.

Anonymous statistics

To improve the game (for example, to see whether the tutorial is clear or which board is too hard), the app sends anonymous events to TelemetryDeck (TelemetryDeck GmbH, Germany, servers in the EU). Examples: a dig started or finished, the number of cave-ins, tutorial steps, the purchase screen being shown, plus device model, system and app version, language and region.

Events are linked only to a random installation identifier that is hashed before it is sent. We never send your name, e-mail address, location or advertising identifier. We do not link this data to your identity and do not track you across other apps. You can turn statistics off at any time: Settings → Privacy.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in developing the game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Turning statistics off is an objection we always respect.

Purchases

Payments are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Statistics only record that a product was bought.

Game Center

Leaderboards and achievements are handled by Apple Game Center according to your Game Center settings and Apple's privacy policy. We do not store this data.

Game content files

The app downloads new daily boards and game settings from a static server. The hosting provider may technically log IP addresses in server logs. We do not use those logs to identify players.

Notifications

The only notification (ticket office full) is scheduled locally on your device. We do not send push notifications.

Children

The game is suitable for everyone and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your rights

You have the right to access, erase and restrict processing of your data, to object, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. Because statistics are anonymous, we usually cannot attribute them to a specific person. You can delete your local progress in Settings (Reset progress) or by deleting the app.

Changes

We will mention significant changes to this policy in the app's update notes.
