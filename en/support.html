FAQ

How do I restore my purchases?

Settings → Purchases → Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with. Purchases also work with Family Sharing.

My progress is gone after reinstalling.

Progress (museum, tickets, digs) is stored only on your device, so deleting the game removes it. Purchases come back with “Restore purchases”.

When does the daily dig change?

At midnight UTC. Missing a day never takes anything away.

How do I turn off statistics?

Settings → Privacy → Anonymous statistics. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I start over?

Settings → Reset progress. Purchases are kept.
